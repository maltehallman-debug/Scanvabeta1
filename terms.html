Your right of withdrawal

If you buy as a consumer in the EU, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase (distansavtalslagen, 2 kap. 10 §). For digital content that is not delivered on a physical medium, that right ends once delivery has started, but only if you expressly agreed to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you lose the right of withdrawal (2 kap. 11 §).
