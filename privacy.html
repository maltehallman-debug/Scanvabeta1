Privacy

Scanva is a training diary that runs as a Google Apps Script project inside your own Google account and reads and writes your own spreadsheet. This page explains what happens to your data on this website, in the live demo, during Quick install, and in the app once it's installed.

Who is responsible

Scanva is made by Malte Hallman. Contact: malte.hallman@gmail.com.

For this website, Malte Hallman is the data controller. Once you install the app, it runs in your Google account and works on your spreadsheet: that data is yours and Scanva has no access to it.

When you visit this website

The website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and may keep them in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts are served from this website itself, not from a third party. The site has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or social widgets, and sets no cookies and nothing in your browser's storage.

The live demo

The demo generates a fictional athlete in your browser. Nothing you enter in the demo is sent anywhere or saved; it's gone when you reload. The demo loads the Leaflet map library from cdnjs (Cloudflare), which receives your IP address when it's downloaded.

Quick install

Google's sign-in library is loaded from accounts.google.com only when you open Quick install. When you choose Continue with Google, Google asks for permission to create and deploy an Apps Script project (script.projects and script.deployments).

  • The access token Google gives your browser is kept in the page's memory only and used to call Google's Apps Script API directly from your browser. It is never sent to Scanva or stored.
  • The spreadsheet link you paste is only used to write your file's ID into the new project's code.
  • The new project is created in your Google account and deployed so that only you can open it.

The spreadsheet template

The "Get the template" link goes to Payhip, an external site. Scanva doesn't sell anything and receives no data from Payhip. Anything you do on Payhip is covered by Payhip's own terms and privacy policy.

Legal basis and transfers

Scanva doesn't collect personal data itself. The server logs GitHub keeps when you visit are needed to run the website (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). GitHub, Google and Cloudflare are based in the US and describe how they protect transfers of personal data in their own privacy statements.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data about you, and to object to processing. Email malte.hallman@gmail.com. Scanva itself doesn't hold data about you, so most requests will be about GitHub, Google, Strava or Payhip, and we'll point you to the right place.

You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If the site or app changes what it does with data, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
